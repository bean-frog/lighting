About My Work

I provide live lighting experiences for events at UO.

All of my shows are busked, meaning that I control the entire rig live, following the DJ. Essentially, I'm doing what the DJ does with music, but with the lighting.

I got started my freshman year, running lighting at the Bridge Raves. I worked one event where it was under the name of The Temple, then worked with Dirt God and Wubsea on BigTop events in the same location.

When I first started, all I had was a StreamDeck and four PARs (static RGB lights), all set up on a big stick as a truss (lol). I have since expanded, and my current setup includes six PARs, four moving lights, and dual tripod stands.

I've had the pleasure of working with a few fraternities, as well as at The Annex for 1STGEN: Rave Oregon.

I'd love to work with you to make your event the best it can be, please reach out to my instagram @bnfg.lighting for pricing inquiries and to discuss.

For fraternity clients, please note that I am unavailable if your event coincides with a Pi Kappa Phi event. If you're unsure, just ask me :)
