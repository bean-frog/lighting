About My Work

I provide live lighting experiences for events at UO.

All of my shows are busked, meaning that I control the entire rig live, following the DJ. Essentially, I'm doing what the DJ does with music, but with the lighting.

I got started my freshman year, running lighting at the Bridge Raves. I worked one event where it was under the name of The Temple, then worked with Dirt God and Wubsea on BigTop events in the same location.

I've had the pleasure of working with a few fraternities, as well as at The Annex for 1STGEN: Rave Oregon.

I'd love to work with you to make your event the best it can be, please reach out to my instagram @bnfg.lighting for pricing inquiries and to discuss.

For fraternity clients, please note that I am unavailable if your event coincides with a Pi Kappa Phi event. If you're unsure, just ask me :)

Lighting Design

Design services at several levels, tailored to your budget. Operation is not included, except at Level 3

  1. 1

    Fixtures & Rigging

    Given your budget and any ideas or input you have, I will research fixtures and create a stage plot and fixture list for your space.

    I'll also design color palettes and effects for the selected fixtures.

    You are responsible for purchasing fixtures and rigging equipment. For a labor fee, I can handle installation.

    $75 design fee, plus $35/hr if you want me to install

  2. 2

    QLC+ Showfile

    Everything in Level 1, plus a showfile mapped to your fixtures in QLC+ (a free DMX control program for Mac, Windows, and Linux).

    Built for automatic effects, with simple controls for effects and colors. Easy to use with a mouse, and QLC+ supports binding buttons to MIDI controllers.

    $150 total (includes Level 1 design; installation is $35/hr)

  3. 3

    Full Service

    The big one: everything from Levels 1 and 2, plus full setup and live busking.

    Depending on your setup from Level 1, I may use only your equipment or, if desired, add my own fixtures to the show.

    You'll still receive the QLC+ showfile, though I'll busk with my own.

    Pricing

    • $150 design fee (covers Levels 1 and 2), plus $35/hr for installation
    • Live busking at the standard rate ($150 + $30/hr), with a package discount (varies)
